A fintech platform needed someone to administer and evolve its AWS environment as the product grew: keeping the infrastructure reliable, containerizing workloads, and owning the pipeline that ships code to production.

## What the work covers

- **Day-to-day AWS administration:** compute, networking and access management.
- **Containers:** application services packaged for consistent, reproducible deployments.
- **CI/CD:** pipelines that build, test and deploy automatically.
- **Operations:** monitoring and incident-response practices suited to a regulated financial environment.
